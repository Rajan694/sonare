## New

- Keep playing similar songs when the queue runs out
- Search suggestions as you type, recent searches and Load more
- Forgot password by emailed code, and an Account screen with your details
- Verify your email with a 6-digit code; the emailed link opens again
- Own playlists in Offline Mode; reorder them, rename them and add songs
- The queue comes back after a restart and follows you between devices
- Gapless playback and crossfade, with the next song looked up ahead (desktop)
- Media controls for the desktop app
- Liked hearts everywhere, and read-only lyrics
- Album and playlist results, offline search and Up next (Android)
- Music folders: the phone's own songs play in Offline Mode (Android)
- Downloads are found again after a reinstall
- A 300 MB stream cache, so a song played again doesn't download again
- In-app YouTube songs start playing while they download (Linux)
- Settings are kept on the device and synced to your account
- Waveforms show loudness, and genre cards show a photo per genre
- Both apps say when the server is waking up
- "What's new" shows a newer build's changes before you download it
- Sonare plays on the system's audio output

## Fixes

- The keyboard no longer covers the field you're typing in (Android)
- Streaming no longer breaks off mid-song (Android)
- sonare.vip stops asking for local network access
- The Library song list scrolls to its end (Linux)
- A song plays while it downloads (Linux)
- A limiter keeps the EQ from clipping (desktop)
- Space and the arrow keys control the player reliably (desktop)
- You stay signed in unless the server really ends the session
- Offline plays upload in batches, so a long backlog isn't lost
- Downloads without tags show their artist and title, not the file name
- An own playlist's header counts the songs that play offline
- "1 song", not "1 songs"
